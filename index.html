The cost of delay

The leaky lead problem

Every contractor has this leak, and it runs two ways. One lead dies waiting in an inbox. The other never makes it that far, because nobody picked up. Both are invisible on a spreadsheet and brutal on a P&L.

Scenario one

The lead that sat in an inbox

Stage 01

The form comes in

12:40 on a Tuesday. A homeowner asks for a quote on tearing out and repouring her driveway.

Fresh
Stage 02

Your hands are full

You're setting forms in the sun with a crew waiting on you. The phone is in the truck and email isn't happening.

Unseen
Stage 03

The inbox buries it

Four hours go by. Supply house invoices and a permit email push it off the first screen.

Going cold
Stage 04

Someone else got it

You open it at 6pm. She booked a guy who called her back at 1:15. You never knew it was a $14,000 job.

Lost

Scenario two

The call you never knew about

Ringing
Stage 02

Missed
Stage 03

Silent
Stage 04

The next guy answers
