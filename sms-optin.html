SMS text alerts

Get LeadIntel alerts by text.

Opt in to receive SMS notifications from LeadIntel CRM: a text the moment a new lead is scored and ready for follow-up, plus account notifications. Optional, and only about your account.

What we send

LeadIntel CRM can send you SMS/text messages about your account: lead alerts (for example, a text when a new lead is scored and ready for follow-up) and related account notifications. These are operational account notifications only, never marketing or promotional messages.

It’s optional

Agreeing to text messages is optional and is not required to sign up for or use LeadIntel CRM. It’s a separate choice you can make, or skip, at any time.

Consent to receive texts is not a condition of signing up for or using LeadIntel CRM.
