About LeadIntel CRM

Built on a job site, not in a boardroom.

LeadIntel CRM was built by a former construction foreman who got tired of watching solid leads disappear to faster competitors, not better ones. Running a crew means you already know your work is good. What you don’t always have is the time to call every lead back in the first five minutes, every time.

So we built the thing that does it for you: AI-powered scoring and instant alerts, so the fastest response is always yours.

What we believe

Three things we won’t compromise on.

Speed is a fairness issue.

Good work shouldn’t lose to good work just because someone else called back ten minutes sooner. Leveling that playing field is the whole point of this product.

Built for the trade, not adapted from a generic CRM.

This isn’t a repurposed sales tool. Every part of it, from the questions the chatbot asks to the way leads get scored, is built around how contracting work actually gets won and lost.

Simple enough to actually use.

You’re running a business, not learning new software. If a feature doesn’t save you real time, it doesn’t make it into the product.
