About LeadIntel CRM

Built on a job site, not in a boardroom.

I spent eight years in construction, three of them running crews, and I watched the same thing happen over and over: solid leads going to faster competitors, not better ones. When you’re running a crew you already know your work is good. What you don’t have is somebody free at 9pm to call back the homeowner who just filled out your form, or to pick up when the next one calls instead.

So I built what I wished I’d had. Every lead gets scored against your actual pricing, your service area, and the work you turn down, the second it lands. The alert hits your inbox and your phone. And every lead stacks up on a dashboard with the best one on top, so you’re never guessing which call to make first.

What we believe

Three things we won’t compromise on.

Speed is a fairness issue.

The best contractor doesn’t always win the job. The fastest one does. A homeowner who fills out four forms on a Tuesday night hires whoever calls back first, and that has nothing to do with who does better work. Closing that gap is the entire point of this product.

Built for the trade, not adapted from a generic CRM.

This isn’t a repurposed sales tool with a contractor logo on it. The questions the chatbot asks, what the voice agent digs for when it answers a call you missed, the way leads get scored, what counts as a bad lead: all of it comes from knowing how this work actually gets won and lost.

Simple enough to actually use.

You’re running a business, not learning software. Log in from your phone with a link, no password to remember. If a feature doesn’t save you real time on a real day, it doesn’t ship.
