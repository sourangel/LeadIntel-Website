Questions

Common questions

Why “starting at” instead of a fixed price?

Lead volume and feature needs vary a lot between a small remodeling crew and a high-volume roofing company. The tiers show you where pricing lands; a quick conversation gets you an exact number that’s actually fair for your business.

Which tier is right for me?

Start with your monthly lead volume, then look at how your customers actually reach you. If a good share of them call instead of filling out a form, you want the voice agent picking up the ones you miss, and that starts at Growth. Same for a text the moment a good lead lands. On the call we’ll match you to the right fit, not the biggest number.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep the number your customers already have and forward it to LeadIntel for the calls you don’t pick up. Calls you answer yourself are untouched, and we set the forwarding up with you during onboarding.

What counts as a voice minute?

Only the time the voice agent actually spends on a call. Calls you answer yourself don’t touch your minutes. If you’re consistently running past what your plan includes, we’ll move you to one that fits rather than let it pile up.

Is there a contract or commitment?

We’ll go over the details on your call. This is intentionally a conversation, not a checkout page.
